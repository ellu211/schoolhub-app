Privacy Policy for SchoolHub

Last updated: 22 November 2026 • Effective immediately

1. Introduction

SchoolHub ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an educational management platform developed for students, parents, teachers, and school administrators. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, FERPA, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection), and relevant local educational regulations.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver academic, attendance, and fee tracking services, the application processes:

  • Student Educational Records: Student identification numbers, full names, enrolled class/section, roll numbers, daily attendance records, and examination marks.
  • Parent & Guardian Information: Parent names, emergency contact numbers, and student relationship.
  • Homework & Files: Submitted assignments, PDF worksheets, and teacher feedback notes.
  • Fee Records: Invoiced fee heads, offline counter payment receipt numbers, and digital transaction receipts. No credit card or debit card numbers are ever stored directly by SchoolHub.
  • Camera & Storage (Optional): Accessed only when a user explicitly chooses to take a photo of an assignment or upload a homework PDF.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

SchoolHub is designed for schools and parents. Student accounts are authorized and managed directly through schools or verified parent accounts. We do not display behavioral advertising, sell student data, or share personal information with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data is transmitted via industry-standard TLS encryption. School records are accessible solely to authenticated teachers, administrators, and the authorized student/parent guardian of that specific pupil.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

Parents and students may review, modify, or request deletion of their records at any time by contacting the school administrative office or by reaching out to our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@schoolhub.app.
